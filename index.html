Add a widget to CarPlay

1. Create and save a design in the app.
2. On iPhone, open Settings › General › CarPlay and choose your car.
3. Open Widgets and add Car Widget Studio.
4. To choose which design a widget shows, long-press the widget on your iPhone and tap Edit Widget.

CarPlay widgets need iOS 26 and a car or stereo with Apple CarPlay.

Common questions

My widget didn't change right away. iOS decides when widgets refresh. It can take a few minutes.

Sounds don't play when CarPlay connects. Sounds are played by a Shortcuts automation you set up once. Follow the guide in Settings › Connect, Disconnect & Arrival Sounds.

Video doesn't show on my car's screen. Parked Video plays on your iPhone and can be sent to an AirPlay display. Showing video on a car screen needs a car that supports AirPlay video, and only works while parked.
