Privacy Policy

Car Widget Studio · Effective November 17, 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

Short version

Car Widget Studio does not collect any data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your widget designs, the photos you choose for them, your sound choices and your settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, so the app never gets access to your photo library.

Network use

The app goes online in one case only, started by you: when you enter a video stream address in Parked Video, your iPhone connects directly to that address to play it. That site has its own privacy practices. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
